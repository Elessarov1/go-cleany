# 0003 — Managed service, not provider catalog

## Decision

The customer delegates a task to Loco. Loco does not make provider comparison/coordination the core product.

## Why

The value proposition is reducing search, uncertainty and coordination burden.

## Consequences

- no default "choose from 20 cleaners/drivers" marketplace UX;
- provider replacement/dispatch is an internal operational concern;
- support ownership stays with Loco;
- product optimizes for successful fulfillment and low customer effort;
- provider quality is important internally even without public ratings.
